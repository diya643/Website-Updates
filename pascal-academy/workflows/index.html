AI Workflows

How AI turns financial work into repeatable processes. See how real financial tasks can be broken down, automated, and run with AI.

Every workflow breaks a financial task into five stages: define the job, gather context, run the process, produce the deliverable, and review the result.

The anatomy of a workflow

Stage 1 Task. What are we trying to accomplish?Define the job clearly. What question needs answering, what decision needs to be supported, and what does a useful result look like?
Stage 2 Context. What do we need to know?Bring together the information required to do the work well: filings, transcripts, financials, market data, prior analysis, internal research, and other relevant sources.
Stage 3 Process. How should the work be done?Define the sequence of steps required to reach the answer. What needs to be retrieved, compared, calculated, tested, or synthesized?
Stage 4 Output. What should the work produce?Turn the analysis into a usable deliverable: a summary, comparison, timeline, model, assessment, or recommendation. The output should support the next action.
Stage 5 Review. Where does judgment come in?A person validates the work, checks the important assumptions, edits where necessary, and makes the final call. Automation handles the process; judgment stays with the analyst.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI workflow in finance?
A sequence of connected tasks used to complete a broader process. A good workflow has a defined task, the right inputs, a structured process, and an output that fits into the work that follows.
What are the five stages of a workflow?
Task, context, process, output and review: define the job, gather context, run the process, produce the deliverable, and review the result.
Which workflows are in the library?
Twenty workflows across earnings, company research, competition, and news and events, each with its stages, inputs and output.
Can I run these workflows in Pascal?
Yes. Open any workflow and choose Try this workflow to see it run in Pascal.
