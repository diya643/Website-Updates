Frequently asked questions

What is Alpha & Agents?
Alpha & Agents is a newsletter on AI in institutional finance, written by Vibhav Viswanathan, founder of Pascal AI Labs. It covers how asset managers, hedge funds, banks and asset servicers adopt AI, what it costs, and what infrastructure it needs.
How often are new essays published?
A new essay goes out about twice a month. Every essay is listed on this page and published in full on Substack.
How do I subscribe?
Open any essay on Substack and subscribe there. New essays arrive by email.
Are the essays about Pascal?
Mostly they are about the industry. Where an essay draws on Pascal deployments, client details are anonymised. The industry pages show how those workflows run today.
Can I share or cite an essay?
Yes. Link to the essay on Substack and credit Alpha & Agents by Pascal AI Labs.
