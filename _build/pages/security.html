Principles

How Pascal handles your data.

  1. 01

    Client data trains nothing

    Prompts and data stay out of model training and refinement. They serve your team only.

  2. 02

    Permissions travel with the data

    Every entity inherits the permissions of its source system, checked per user and per source. See the context graph

  3. 03

    Every action is logged

    Every query, answer and action is recorded in an audit log and can be traced. See Pascal Agents

  4. 04

    Every claim is cited

    Each output carries source citations, auditable back to the filing, transcript or document. See cited research

  5. 05

    Approved models only

    Pascal routes each task to the best-suited LLM from the models your firm approves.

  6. 06

    Deployment on your terms

    Run Pascal on a dedicated instance, in your VPC or on-premises, wherever your controls require.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pascal train models on our data?
Never. Pascal does not use client prompts or data to train or refine models. Your data only serves your team.
Which security certifications does Pascal hold?
Pascal is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. GDPR is coming soon.
Can Pascal run inside our own environment?
Yes. Pascal can be deployed on-premises or inside your VPC on AWS, Azure or GCP. Dedicated instances are also available, with restricted or full features.
How is our data encrypted?
With AES-256 in transit and at rest. Encryption covers storage, file transmission, networks, systems and internal protocols.
How does Pascal decide who can see what?
Role-based access control is enforced at the data level. Pascal checks permissions per user and per source before anything reaches an analyst or an agent, and every entity inherits the permissions of its source system.
Can we audit what Pascal did and why?
Yes. Every query, answer and action is logged and traceable, and every output carries source citations back to the filing, transcript or document.
