Frequently asked questions

What does an AI transformation partner do for a financial institution?
Pascal maps a team's workflow and data, builds the workflow on the firm's own context graph, deploys it inside the firm's perimeter and then repeats the process for the next team. The firm gets software in production and a team that stays with it after go-live.
How long does it take to get the first workflow live?
About six weeks from kick-off for a typical first workflow, such as event briefs for research or pre-meeting briefs for sales. Later workflows reuse the same connectors and permissions, so they go live faster.
Which functions do firms add after research?
Sales and marketing intelligence usually come next, because they read the same research and CRM data. Underwriting, operations, compliance and investor relations follow, and corporates add treasury and hedge accounting.
Do we have to replace the AI tools we already use?
No. Pascal runs the models you approve, and its MCP server brings your context graph into the assistants your teams already use, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Pascal workflows can also run as tools inside them.
How do you know a workflow is working?
Each workflow has an evaluation set agreed with the team before go-live, rerun whenever a model or prompt changes. Reviewer edits and usage are tracked per team, so you can see where a workflow earns its place.
Who owns the workflows and the data?
You do. The context graph, the workflows and the evaluation sets sit in your environment, under your access controls, and stay there if the programme ends.
