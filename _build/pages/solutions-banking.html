Frequently asked questions

How do banks and lenders use AI across front, middle and back office?
In the front office, lenders use AI to draft credit memos and prepare relationship managers for client meetings. In the middle office, it reviews credit risk, tests covenants across the loan book and checks KYC files. In the back office, it builds regulatory returns, checks loan documents against sanction terms and supports legal review. Pascal runs all three on one context graph, with every figure linked to its source.
How does Pascal support credit underwriting?
Pascal reads the borrower pack: audited financials, bank statements, GST or tax returns, rating reports and the relationship manager's site-visit notes. It spreads the financials into your template, ties bank-statement credits to reported revenue, runs your policy checks and drafts the credit memo with each figure linked to its page. The underwriter reviews the draft and the credit committee makes the decision.
Can Pascal check a memo against our own credit policy?
Yes. Your credit policy is loaded as a set of rules with its version number, such as coverage floors, debt limits, concentration caps, working-capital cycles and rating thresholds. Every draft memo is tested against the current version, and each exception is listed with the clause it breaks and any mitigant found in the file.
Can Pascal prepare relationship managers for client meetings?
Yes. Before a meeting, Pascal reads the client's CRM history, recent filings and news, current limits and utilisation, and any open credit or KYC actions. It writes a one-page brief with talking points and the renewals or covenant tests coming up, each line linked to its source.
Does Pascal work for retail lenders and NBFCs?
Yes. Retail, MSME and specialist lenders use the same workflows on their own files: loan applications, income documents and bank statements read and checked against product-level credit rules, with exceptions routed to the underwriter. Each product keeps its own policy version, so a gold loan and a business loan are tested against different rules.
How does Pascal handle borrower data and audit requests?
Pascal deploys in your private cloud or on premise, so borrower and KYC data stays inside the bank. Access follows your existing entitlements. Every memo, check and return keeps a log of the sources read, the rules applied and who approved each edit, ready for internal audit and the regulator.
