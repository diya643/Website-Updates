Frequently asked questions

What is a context graph?
A knowledge graph of the companies, segments, KPIs, theses, people and decisions your firm works with, each linked to the document it came from. Ask Pascal, Pascal Agents and the Pascal MCP all read from it.
How does institutional memory relate to the context graph?
They are one product. Your firm's memory, from IC memos and models to meeting decisions and approved answers, is stored in the graph, so anything Pascal learns once is available to every user and agent cleared to see it.
How do decisions get into the graph?
From meeting notes Pascal reads through your connectors, and from reviewer edits to Pascal's work, such as a compliance change to a DDQ answer. Each decision is stored with its source, date and owner.
Can a decision be corrected or retired?
Yes. The owner or an admin can edit or retire any decision. The graph keeps the history, and agents pick up the change on their next run.
Do we have to move our data into Pascal?
No. Connectors read SharePoint, email, Teams, CRM and your market data providers where they are, and Pascal can be deployed inside your own virtual private cloud.
Who can see what is in the graph?
Every entity and decision inherits the permissions of its source. Pascal checks them per user and per source before anything reaches an analyst or an agent.
