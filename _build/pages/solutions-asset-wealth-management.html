Frequently asked questions

How do asset and wealth managers use AI across front, middle and back office?
In the front office, analysts and fund managers use AI for company research and earnings recaps, and wealth teams use it for client portfolio reviews and model portfolio commentary. In the middle office, it checks holdings against mandate limits and writes attribution commentary. In the back office, it drafts RFP answers, factsheets, client reports and regulatory returns. Pascal runs all of it on one context graph, so every team quotes the same numbers.
Can Pascal write earnings recaps in our house template?
Yes. Pascal learns your recap template from past examples: the section order, the variance table, the tone your CIO expects. For each result it reads the filing, the call transcript and broker notes, compares the numbers with your analyst's model and writes the recap in that template, with every figure cited to its page.
How does Pascal check a portfolio against its offer document or mandate?
Pascal reads the investment limits from the offer document, scheme information document or client mandate and keeps each one with its clause reference. It then checks the daily holdings file against every limit. A breach comes with the clause, the holding behind it, the cause (a trade or a passive move from flows) and the rebalancing window the document allows. See autonomous agents for how checks run on a schedule.
How do wealth advisors and relationship managers use Pascal?
Advisors ask Pascal to prepare a client portfolio review from the holdings file, the house view and the latest model portfolio commentary. Pascal drafts the review in the advisor template, explains each change in plain language and cites every number, so the advisor walks into the meeting with a document compliance has already seen the sources for.
Can Pascal answer RFPs and consultant questionnaires?
Yes. Pascal matches each question to your approved answer library, refreshes performance and AUM figures from the latest files and drafts new answers from your policies. Reviewer edits go back into the library, so the next consultant questionnaire starts from the approved wording.
Does client and portfolio data stay inside our perimeter?
Yes. Pascal deploys in your private cloud or on premise. Access follows your existing permissions, so an advisor sees only the client books they cover and a fund manager sees only their own funds' holdings.
