Frequently asked questions

How does Pascal manage my data?
AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest, SOC 2 Type II infrastructure, strict access controls and an audit log on every action. Pascal can run on-prem or inside your VPC.
Can I upload my own data to Pascal?
Yes. PDFs, Excel, PowerPoint, Word and images. Pascal extracts and analyses each file while preserving its original structure, and the Pascal Data Agent turns them into structured, entity-tagged data.
How does Pascal fit into existing workflows?
Pascal works in Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook, Teams and Slack, connects to SharePoint and your data providers, and brings your context into any LLM through MCP. Teams keep their existing habits, from the front office to the back office.
How does Pascal use my data?
Pascal does not use your prompts or data for model training. Your data only serves your team, and every user sees only what their role allows.
How quickly can my team get started with Pascal?
Full deployment takes under four weeks, including connecting your systems and setting permissions.
What kind of accuracy can I expect from Pascal?
Every output carries source citations, and every claim is auditable back to the filing, transcript or document it came from.
