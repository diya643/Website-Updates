Frequently asked questions

What are Pascal Agents?
AI agents that run institutional work end to end. Give them a task and they plan it, search your firm's research and the market, check what they find, and deliver the finished brief, model, record or deck.
Are agents only for investment research?
No. Research is the most common use, but agents also run middle- and back-office work: trade breaks, reconciliations, NAV exceptions, DDQ answers, KYC packs and investor reporting.
Does the Excel agent change how our models are built?
No. It learns how your team builds them, asks when it needs a decision, and follows those rules from then on.
Can I see and stop what an agent is doing?
Yes. Every run shows its steps live, and you can stop it at any point.
