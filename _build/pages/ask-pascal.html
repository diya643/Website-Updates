Frequently asked questions

What is Ask Pascal?
Ask Pascal answers with everything your firm has learned: your filings and data, your IC memos and models, your email and Teams threads, and the decisions your team has made. Every answer is sourced and grounded in your firm's own context.
Is Ask Pascal only for investment research?
No. Investment teams use it most, but risk, operations, compliance, IC and IR teams use the same sourced answers from the same institutional memory.
Does everyone see the same information?
No. Pascal respects your firm's access controls, so each person only sees what they are cleared to see.
Can it use our own documents?
Yes. Connect your systems or upload files, and Pascal cites them exactly as it cites a public filing.
