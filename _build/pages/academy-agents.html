Every Pascal agent follows the same pattern. You define what you need, the agent gathers and processes the inputs, and you get a structured output with citations back to the source.

Step 01

Select your inputs

Choose companies, filings, documents, or your own uploaded research. The agent works with public filings and your internal files side by side.

Step 02

Define what you need

Pick a template, specify a question, or select which KPIs to extract. The agent's guided setup walks you through the options.

Step 03

Get a cited output

The agent processes the inputs and returns a structured result. Every number, claim, and data point traces back to the source line it came from.

Frequently asked questions

What are AI agents for finance?
AI agents for finance are purpose-built tools that handle specific investment research tasks. They filter company universes, extract financial data from filings, and generate structured research reports. Unlike general AI chatbots, finance agents are designed around investment workflows and return cited, auditable outputs.
What is Pascal AI and what does it do?
Pascal AI is an institutional finance platform that provides AI agents for investment research. It runs inside your governance perimeter with role-based access controls and citation trails on every output. Pascal offers agents for company screening, KPI extraction from filings, and research memo generation, along with a library of pre-built templates for company primers, earnings analysis, and credit memos.
How do AI agents extract financial data from filings?
AI agents like Pascal's KPI Extractor read company filings including annual reports, quarterly reports, and earnings presentations, then identify and extract tables, graphs, and key metrics into a structured format. The agent selects the company, source document, and reporting period, then returns the extracted data in a format that can be exported to Excel.
Can AI generate investment research reports?
Yes. AI agents can generate structured research reports using predefined templates. Pascal's Research Memo Generator takes selected sources and a chosen template, then produces a formatted report with sections, data points, and citations. Templates include company primers, earnings analysis, IPO prospectus summaries, annual report summaries, and credit memos.
How do AI agents handle data security in finance?
Pascal's agents run inside your governance perimeter with role-based access controls. Every output includes citations tracing back to the source line, making results auditable. The platform does not expose sensitive data to consumer AI tools and operates within enterprise data agreements and compliance frameworks.
