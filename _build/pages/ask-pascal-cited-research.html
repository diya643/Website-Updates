Frequently asked questions

What is cited research?
Ask about any company and every sentence in the answer links to the exact page of the document it came from, whether that is a 10-K, an earnings deck, a broker note or your own IC memo. Click a citation and the source opens beside the answer.
What can Pascal cite?
Public filings, earnings presentations, transcripts and exchange disclosures, plus your connected internal documents and anything you upload to your Vault.
Can I ask about more than one company at once?
Yes. Add a second @ and Pascal searches both companies and cites each separately.
Do citations work on numbers in tables?
Yes. Summary tables include a source column, so every figure links back to its page.
