Frequently asked questions

What is Pascal Academy?
Practical AI education for modern investment teams: desk guides for hedge funds, asset managers, bankers and private equity, a ten-part AI fundamentals series, and a library of AI terms, workflows, prompts and agents.
Who is Pascal Academy for?
Portfolio managers, analysts, heads of research, bankers and private markets teams, and the technology and operations leaders who support them.
Can I download the guides as PDFs?
Yes. Every desk guide and AI fundamentals guide has a Download the PDF button; enter your work email and we send the PDF to your inbox.
Does Pascal Academy run workshops for teams?
Yes. The Pascal team runs live workshops planned around your firm. Sign up and we will follow up to plan one.
