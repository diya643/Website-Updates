Frequently asked questions

What is the Pascal AI platform?
An AI platform for investment firms. It combines a context graph of your firm's knowledge with document intelligence, and runs Ask Pascal and Pascal Agents on top, with the Pascal MCP for any other LLM.
How is Pascal different from ChatGPT or Copilot?
Pascal answers from your firm's own context, checks permissions per user and per source, and cites every claim to the page.
Can Pascal run in our own cloud?
Yes. Pascal deploys in your VPC on AWS, Azure or GCP with the models your firm approves.
Which systems does Pascal connect to?
SharePoint, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Google Drive, Box, Notion, CRM and research management systems, plus filings, transcripts and market data from providers such as Quartr, Accord Fintech and Crustdata.
