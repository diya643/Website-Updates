Frequently asked questions

How is Pascal deployed at a financial firm?
Pascal runs in your private cloud or on premise, inside your perimeter. It connects to the sources you already use, such as SharePoint, email, research feeds and filings, and applies your existing permissions to every user and agent.
How long does a Pascal deployment take?
The five deployments on this page went live in four to ten weeks. Each started with one workflow on the client's own documents and grew from there.
How are the results in these case studies measured?
Each result compares the same workflow before and after Pascal, measured with the client's team: time to finish the task, volume processed and the share of output accepted without edits.
Why are the clients anonymous?
Financial firms rarely let vendors name them. Each story describes the firm by type and size, and names and logos are added as clients approve them.
Where should our firm start?
Pick one workflow that costs your team days each month, such as recaps, RFPs, comps or reconciliations, and measure it against today's baseline. See the workflow library for common starting points.
