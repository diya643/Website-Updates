Frequently asked questions

What is Pascal Data Agent?
The part of Pascal that turns your firm's files into structured data. It classifies each file, extracts the fields that matter for its type, links them to your context graph and serves them to analysts and agents.
Which files and sources does it work with?
Broker research portals such as BluMatrix, expert network transcripts, SharePoint, OneDrive and other drives, and email with attachments. PDFs, Word files, slides and scanned documents are all supported.
Is Pascal a data company?
Pascal is a data platform for your own research, the way Snowflake is a data platform for your tables. The data is your firm's; Pascal structures it, keeps it current and serves it.
How do agents use the data?
Through Pascal's MCP server. Agents call list_documents to filter the vault and get_document to read the section about a company, from any MCP client your firm approves.
Does it respect our permissions?
Yes. Every list, count, answer and MCP call follows the user's source permissions and licences, including per-broker research entitlements.
Where does the data live?
Inside your deployment. Pascal runs in your own cloud or on premise, so the structured data and the original files stay within your perimeter.
