The challenge

The research was there. Nobody could find it.

Broker PDFs arrived through the fund's research feed and in analysts' inboxes. Expert-call transcripts sat in a shared folder, and internal notes lived on SharePoint. Drive and File Explorer search matched file names only, and file names rarely named a company.

Analysts kept private folders and asked colleagues to forward reports they knew existed. One analyst asked for a single repository: every document the fund owns, findable by company, broker and date.

What Pascal did

Five steps. One research vault.

The Pascal Data Agent ran on the fund's own files inside its private cloud. Each step shows the screen the team worked in.

01Classify

Type every document.

Pascal read each file and typed it as a broker report, an expert call or an internal research note. Anything it could not place stayed unclassified, with a suggested type and a confidence score for a person to confirm.

Pull out the fields analysts search by.

Results

Frequently asked questions

How does Pascal classify broker reports and expert-call transcripts?
Pascal reads the content of each file, including its layout, headers and disclosures, and assigns a document type with a confidence score. File names and folders are ignored, which is why it works on archives with inconsistent naming.
What happens to documents Pascal cannot classify?
They stay unclassified and go to a review queue with a suggested type and a confidence score. At this fund, 14 of 6,711 documents needed a person to confirm the type.
Does the research vault respect broker entitlements?
Yes. Reports from brokers the fund does not license and notes restricted to one team are hidden from everyone else, in the vault viewer and over MCP. Users see a count of hidden documents.
Can our own AI tools search the vault?
Yes. Any MCP client can call list_documents and get_document, with the same filters and permissions analysts have in the vault viewer.
How long did the deployment take?
Five weeks from kickoff to the full archive being searchable. The 4,212 broker reports were tagged in week one. Pascal ran inside the fund's private cloud throughout.
