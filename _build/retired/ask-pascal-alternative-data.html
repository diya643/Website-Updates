Why it matters

Results tell you what happened. Indicators tell you what's happening.

By the time demand shows up in a quarterly result, the market has often moved. Monthly indicators let you test a thesis while the quarter is still running, and the data hunting that used to span ten tabs now happens in one place.

Frequently asked questions

What is Macro Pulse?
Macro Pulse brings indicators such as air traffic, vehicle registrations, digital payments, fund flows and industrial output into one cross-sector view, updated monthly, with month-on-month and year-on-year change on every metric.
How often is the data updated?
Monthly, with month-on-month and year-on-year change shown on every metric.
Can I export the data?
Yes. Download any view, or all indicators at once.
Can I search for a specific indicator?
Yes. Search by name, or browse by sector and lens.
