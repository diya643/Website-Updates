Frequently asked questions

How do investment banks use AI across front, middle and back office?
Deal teams use AI to build comps, precedents, pitch books, teasers and CIMs, and research analysts use it to draft results notes. The control room and compliance use it to clear conflicts, screen deal parties and log wall-crossings. Legal, cosec, business information and presentation teams use it for minutes, filings, research requests and pages in house style. Pascal runs all three on one context graph.
Can deal teams build comps and pitch books in our house templates?
Yes. Pascal builds trading comps, precedent transactions and pitch pages in your Excel workbooks and PowerPoint master from filings, investor presentations, deal announcements and the market data you license. Each cell links to its source page, and your past decks guide the layout.
How does Pascal help sell-side research analysts?
Pascal drafts results notes and initiation sections from filings, call transcripts and the analyst's own model, with each claim cited. Before publication, the draft is checked for required disclosures, rating and target price consistency, and names on the restricted list.
How does Pascal support conflict clearance and the control room?
For each new mandate, Pascal screens the deal parties against your conflicts register, research coverage, watch and restricted lists, and sanctions and PEP sources. It lists each finding with its evidence, logs wall-crossings and insider-list changes, and drafts the clearance memo. The control room makes the decision.
How does Pascal respect information barriers?
Pascal deploys in your private cloud or on premise and applies your entitlements to every user and agent. Private-side deal folders stay with the deal team, research stays on the public side, and a wall-crossed analyst sees only the project they were brought over for.
Where should an investment bank start with Pascal?
Most banks start with comps and pitch pages for one coverage team, on their own templates and filings, then add precedents, conflict checks and cosec work on the same graph. See the workflow library for the full list.
