Frequently asked questions

What is institutional memory at an investment firm?
The research, judgement and decisions a firm builds up: IC memos, models, expert calls, broker research, notes and email. Pascal makes them answerable with page-level citations.
Do we have to move our data into Pascal?
No. Pascal connects to your systems where they are, and can be deployed inside your own virtual private cloud.
How does Pascal keep proprietary research private?
Pascal checks permissions per user and per source before anything reaches an analyst or an agent, and never mixes your research with another firm's.
How does it relate to the context graph?
Institutional memory is where your sources come in. The context graph is how Pascal structures them, linking companies, theses, people and decisions.
