The challenge

Hedge accounting ran on spreadsheets and email.

Group treasury hedges FX and commodity exposure for several businesses: dollar and euro imports and exports, and metal and energy purchases. Each hedge needs designation documentation under Ind AS 109 and an effectiveness test every quarter.

Deal terms were retyped from bank confirmations, and exposures arrived as spreadsheets from each business. Quarter-end took three weeks of matching, testing and gathering evidence, and the trail from a journal back to its confirmation lived in inboxes.

What Pascal did

The hedge lifecycle, end to end.

01Read

Read confirmations and ISDA/CSA terms.

Pascal read each bank confirmation as it arrived and extracted the deal terms: instrument, notional, rate, dates and counterparty. From the ISDA and CSA agreements it took thresholds, minimum transfer amounts and eligible collateral.

Draft designation documentation under Ind AS 109.

Frequently asked questions

Can Pascal prepare hedge documentation under Ind AS 109 and IFRS 9?
Yes. Pascal drafts designation documentation in your format with the elements the standard requires: risk management objective, hedged item, hedging instrument, hedge ratio and the economic relationship. Your treasury team reviews and signs each memo.
Which effectiveness test methods does Pascal support?
Pascal runs the methods in your hedge accounting policy, such as critical terms match, dollar offset and regression, and measures ineffectiveness for each relationship.
How does Pascal read bank confirmations and ISDA/CSA terms?
Pascal extracts deal terms from confirmations as they arrive by email or file, and reads thresholds, minimum transfer amounts and eligible collateral from ISDA and CSA agreements, each linked to its page.
Does Pascal post journal entries to our ERP?
Pascal proposes the entries and attaches the evidence. Group accounts approves them before they are posted, so control stays with your team.
What do auditors receive at quarter-end?
An audit pack with every designation memo, test workpaper and journal, each linked to the bank confirmation and exposure behind it.
