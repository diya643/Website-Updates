The problem

Reading time is the binding constraint.

Every quarter brings a new stack of transcripts and filings. Nobody can read them all, so idea discovery quietly narrows to the companies you already know. Screening turns that pile into something you can query.

Keyword screenerWhat companies are saying, across every transcript.
Earnings screenerYour themes, updated as each company reports.
Fundamental screenerWhat companies are reporting, filtered on the numbers.

Keyword screener

Hear a whole industry at once.

Type a theme like tariff impact, margin expansion or capex plans, pick an industry and a period, and every mention comes back in one place. A trend you suspect is building becomes a five-minute search.

Words and numbers

An idea worth working clears both.

A company talking about margin expansion is interesting. A company talking about margin expansion whose numbers already show it is a candidate for the watchlist. Run the keyword screen, pass the survivors through the fundamental screen, and you have a discovery loop that used to take a junior analyst and a fortnight.

Frequently asked questions

What are Pascal's screeners?
Pascal's screeners let you query every transcript for a theme, track that theme as companies report, filter the universe on the numbers, and check what management promised against what it delivered.
What does the keyword screener search?
Earnings call transcripts across the industry and time period you choose, with each mention linked to its passage.
Does the earnings screener update on its own?
Yes. Once saved, the screen updates with what each company said as it reports.
Can I run guidance vs actuals on any company?
Yes. Pick a company and Pascal builds the full report, with a score for the quarter and what to watch next.
