Frequently asked questions

What is a decision trace?
A saved record of a call your team made, such as a confirmed match, a model layout or a ranked thesis point. Pascal applies it the next time the same situation comes up.
Can decisions be changed later?
Yes. Any trace can be reviewed, edited or removed, and thesis points can be re-ranked or added at any time.
Does Pascal know decisions made before it was deployed?
It learns from what was recorded: memos, models, emails, Teams and recorded calls. Unrecorded conversations start being captured from day one.
Can two people hold different views on the same stock?
Yes. Each PM's or team's thesis is kept separately, so new information is checked against the right view.
