How It Works

Build for the task.

Every Pascal agent follows the same pattern. You define what you need, the agent gathers and processes the inputs, and you get a structured output with citations back to the source.

Step 01
Select your inputs

Choose companies, filings, documents, or your own uploaded research. The agent works with public filings and your internal files side by side.

Step 02
Define what you need

Pick a template, specify a question, or select which KPIs to extract. The agent's guided setup walks you through the options.

Step 03
Get a cited output

The agent processes the inputs and returns a structured result. Every number, claim, and data point traces back to the source line it came from.
