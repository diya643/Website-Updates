Frequently asked questions

What is Pascal Intel?
Pascal Intel follows X, Reddit and podcasts on YouTube for the companies and themes you track, and turns them into one dated summary per story, with every source a click away.
Can I choose whose views are weighted?
Yes. Add specific handles to a tracker and their posts are weighted in the summary.
How often do trackers update?
You set the frequency for each tracker when you create it.
What's coming next?
Custom themes, private company coverage and trackers built on a topic alone, without tying them to a company.
