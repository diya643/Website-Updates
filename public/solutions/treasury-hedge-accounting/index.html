Frequently asked questions

How do corporate treasury teams use AI for hedge accounting?
Dealers use it to read exposures from contracts and forecasts and to compare bank quotes. Treasury risk uses it for effectiveness tests and limit checks. Accounting uses it for designation memos, journal entries and audit packs. Pascal runs all three on one hedge register, so each team reads the same record.
Which hedge accounting standards does Pascal support?
Pascal drafts documentation and tests under IFRS 9, Ind AS 109 and ASC 815, in your own templates. Effectiveness thresholds, such as an 80–125% dollar offset band, come from your treasury policy and can differ by entity or standard.
What goes into a hedge designation memo?
The hedged item, the hedging instrument, the risk management objective and strategy, the hedge ratio, the method for assessing effectiveness and the expected sources of ineffectiveness. Pascal fills each field from the deal ticket, the bank confirmation and your policy, and cites the source.
Which treasury systems does Pascal connect to?
Pascal reads your treasury management system and ERP exports, bank confirmations and valuation statements, ISDA and CSA documents, contracts on SharePoint and email. Market data you already license can be added for mark-to-market checks.
Can our external auditors review Pascal's work?
Yes. Each hedge relationship has an audit pack with the signed memo, the confirmation match, the test results and the entries. Auditors get read-only access, and every figure links back to the document it came from.
Where should a corporate treasury start with Pascal?
Most teams start with hedge documentation and confirmation matching on the hedges already on the register, then add quarter-end effectiveness testing and exposure identification. See the workflow library for the full list.
