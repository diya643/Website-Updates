Frequently asked questions

How do asset servicers use AI across front, middle and back office?
Client-facing teams use AI to answer RFPs, due diligence questionnaires and client queries from approved material. Operations teams use it to read corporate action notices and explain settlement fails and reconciliation breaks. Fund accounting, transfer agency and compliance use it for NAV checks, investor queries, KYC packs and control evidence. Pascal runs all of it on one context graph, so each team sees the same record.
Who uses Pascal at a custodian or fund administrator?
The servicer's own teams: relationship managers and the RFP team, corporate actions and settlements, fund accounting, transfer agency and compliance. Pascal runs inside the servicer's environment and works on the notices, files and client records those teams already handle.
How does Pascal process corporate action notices?
Pascal reads each issuer, exchange or depository notice and extracts the event terms: event type, ex-date, record date, pay date, ratio or rate, and election options and deadlines. It compares those terms across every source you receive, flags any field that differs and drafts the client notification once the terms are confirmed. See extraction for how fields are pulled with a link to the source line.
Can Pascal help with NAV production and pricing exceptions?
Yes. Pascal checks vendor prices and NAV moves against your tolerance rules, then explains each exception: a stale price, a corporate action on ex-date, a missing valuation. Where your team has fixed the same exception before, it proposes that fix with the history attached, and the fund accountant approves it.
Does Pascal keep each client's data separate?
Yes. Pascal applies your entitlements per client, fund and team before any document reaches a person or an agent. A relationship manager sees only the clients they cover, and data from one client never appears in another client's answer. Pascal deploys in your private cloud or on premise.
Can Pascal assemble SOC and control evidence for auditors?
Yes. Pascal maps each control in your SOC 1 or ISAE 3402 scope to the evidence your teams already produce: reconciliation sign-offs, access reviews, exception logs. It assembles the evidence pack for the period with a link to every file, so the auditor's sample requests are answered from the record.
