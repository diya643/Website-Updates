Frequently asked questions

How do private equity and private credit funds use AI across front, middle and back office?
Deal teams use AI to screen CIMs, run data-room diligence and draft IC memos, and credit teams use it for credit memos and covenant extraction. Portfolio teams use it to read reporting packs, compare KPIs with budget and test covenant headroom. Investor relations and fund finance use it for LP letters, DDQs and capital notices. Pascal runs all three on one context graph, so the memo, the monitor and the LP letter share the same sources.
Can Pascal read an entire virtual data room?
Yes. Pascal classifies every file synced or exported from your virtual data room, reads contracts, QoE reports, financial statements and policies, and answers diligence questions with the file and page cited. It also drafts seller questions and keeps the Q&A tracker in step with what the data room says.
How does Pascal extract covenants from a credit agreement?
Pascal reads the agreement clause by clause and pulls financial covenants, the EBITDA definition and its add-backs, baskets, pricing and reporting duties into a table, each linked to its clause. Each quarter it tests the borrower's compliance certificate against those definitions and shows the headroom.
Can Pascal answer LP DDQs and operational due diligence requests?
Yes. Pascal drafts answers to ILPA-style DDQs and ODD questionnaires from your approved answer library, LPA, policies and prior responses. Reviewer edits are saved, so the next DDQ starts from the approved wording, with the source of each answer shown for compliance.
How does Pascal keep deal information confidential?
Pascal deploys in your private cloud or on premise. Each data room, deal folder and LP file keeps its own permissions, so a deal team only sees the deals it is on and LP data stays with investor relations and fund finance.
Where should a private equity or credit fund start with Pascal?
Most funds start with one live workflow on their own documents, usually CIM screening or covenant monitoring, then add LP reporting on the same graph. See the workflow library for the full list.
