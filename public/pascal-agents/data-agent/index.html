      Frequently asked questions

      What is Pascal Data Agent?
      The part of Pascal Agents that turns your firm's files into structured data. It classifies each file, extracts the fields and tables that matter for its type, links them to your context graph and serves them to analysts and agents.
      Which files and sources does it work with?
      Broker research portals such as BluMatrix, expert network transcripts, SharePoint, OneDrive and other drives, and email with attachments. PDFs, including scanned ones, Word files, slides and spreadsheets are all supported.
      Is Pascal a data company?
      Pascal is a data platform for your own research, the way Snowflake is a data platform for your tables. The data is your firm's; Pascal structures it, keeps it current and serves it.
      How does Pascal decide which company a file is about?
      It counts tickers, name variants and product lines mapped to each company, then marks the companies a file is about and the ones it only mentions. "Why this is tagged" in the vault viewer lists every signal.
      Can we use our own document types and fields?
      Yes. Rename, split or add types, and give each one its own fields, access rules and threshold. A new type needs about a dozen labelled examples to start, and you mark which fields a person must verify.
      What happens when Pascal is unsure or wrong?
      Files below your confidence threshold wait in the review queue, and low-confidence cells are flagged in the table editor. When someone assigns a type or corrects a value, Pascal keeps the original, who changed it and when, and every agent reads the corrected value.
      How do agents use the data?
      Through Pascal's MCP server. Agents call list_documents with the same filters as the vault viewer and get_document for the section about a company, including the page and position of every value.
      Does it respect our permissions?
      Yes. Every list, count, answer and MCP call follows the user's source permissions and licences, including per-broker research entitlements. Pascal runs in your own cloud or on premise, so the data stays within your perimeter.
