Frequently asked questions

What is the agentic workflow library?
25+ ready-to-run investment workflows. Pick a workflow, pick a company, and Pascal's agents produce a finished piece of work, such as an event brief or an initiating coverage report, in the same structure for every name you cover.
Can workflows run on private companies?
Yes. Private company workflows use filings plus deep web search.
Are workflow outputs cited?
Yes. Every output cites its sources, down to the page.
Can we build our own?
Yes. Use the workflow builder to turn your firm's process into a workflow your team can run.
