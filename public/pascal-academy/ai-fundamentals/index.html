Frequently asked questions

What does the AI fundamentals series cover?
Ten guides: large language models, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents, context windows, knowledge graphs, hallucinations, reasoning models, embeddings and the Model Context Protocol.
Do I need a technical background?
No. The guides are beginner-friendly, with optional Go deeper sections for readers who want the technical detail.
In what order should I read the guides?
In series order. Each part builds on the ones before it, starting with large language models.
Can I take the guides with me?
Yes. Every guide has a Download the PDF button; enter your work email and we send it to your inbox.
